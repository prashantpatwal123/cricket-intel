**Subject:** Licence for using Cricsheet match data in a public cricket statistics app

Hello,

Thank you for Cricsheet. I'm building **CRICINTEL**, a cricket statistics app for the web and mobile web, and I'd like to confirm how I may use your data before releasing anything publicly.

**What it is.** CRICINTEL turns ball-by-ball records into player analysis: how players score and get out, batter v bowler match-ups, career timelines, leaderboards, plain-English questions answered from the data, and a "what happens next?" game based on historical moments. It labels clearly what is observed in the data and what is derived or modelled, and it does not invent tracking, shot or fielding data.

**What data it uses.**
- The JSON match files (format 1.2.0) from your downloads page: IPL, WPL, men's and women's T20Is, and men's and women's ODIs.
- The people register (`people.csv`, `names.csv`), which I understand is under ODC-By 1.0.
- Your coverage and missing-match pages, so I can show users where the data is incomplete.

I download the files unmodified from cricsheet.org, verify checksums, and record where and when each file was retrieved.

**What it publishes.** Derived statistics and visuals: aggregates, percentiles, charts, leaderboards, and views of individual deliveries (for example "over 14.3: 4 runs"). It does not offer the raw files for download.

**Intended availability.** It is currently a private internal preview. I'd like to make it publicly available on the web and mobile web. I may later commercialise it (for example with a paid tier). Nothing is public or commercial today, and nothing will be until I hear from you.

**Attribution.** I propose a visible credit on every page and in the app's About section, linking to cricsheet.org, for example: *"Ball-by-ball data from Cricsheet (cricsheet.org)."* For the register, I would add the ODC-By 1.0 attribution. I'm happy to use any wording you prefer.

**My questions:**
1. May I use the match data to publish **derived statistics and visualisations** in a **publicly available** app?
2. Is that still permitted if the app becomes **commercial**?
3. May I **redistribute raw match data**, for example by showing individual delivery records, or offering exports? (If not, I will keep raw data private and publish derived output only.)
4. What **attribution wording and placement** would you like?
5. Is there a licence I should cite for the match data, or any conditions I should follow?

If any of this is not something you're comfortable with, I'll adjust the plan. I'm also happy to share a preview link.
