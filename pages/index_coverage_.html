What do we mean by coverage?

In an ideal world we’d have a data file for every match in the periods we’re attempting to cover. Sadly this isn’t always possible, therefore we have some gaps. This page attempts to give an idea of periods we’re attempting to cover, and the percentage of matches we have, for a selection of the match types and teams we currently cover. You can see full details below on the periods we attempt to cover, and the various percentages for different types of matches.

You might notice that coverage for Women’s cricket is less extensive than for the Men’s. This is due to it being harder to source the data for Women’s cricket. We’re trying, and we do include matches where we can source the data. If you can help with any data from these matches we’d love to here from you!
